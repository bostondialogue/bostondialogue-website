About Us

Serving Greater Boston since 2000

Mission

Why We Exist

We enrich our resources by networking with people from many different backgrounds who live in the Greater Boston area to work together to understand and help each other.

We complete our mission by creating and running multihued activities and programs in six main areas: Education, Dialogue, Humanitarian aid, Advocacy, Religious and Community Services.

Vision

What We're Building Toward

We create inclusive programs in various areas that anyone and everyone can participate in, adding value to the Greater Boston area.

Goals

How We Get There

  • 1Facilitate unity for building peace, education to eradicate ignorance, welfare to fight against poverty and hunger, progress to promote development.
  • 2Build relationships among diverse cultures and traditions.
  • 3Unite different points of view on common issues.
  • 4Provide educational platforms for social challenges.
  • 5Encourage people to actively engage in solving social and global problems of humanity.
  • 6Provide an atmosphere of peace and understanding for all people, regardless of race and cultural tradition.
  • 7Build a peaceful, nurturing, and constructive environment for members of different faiths, cultures, ethnicities, and world views to exchange ideas and solve common problems including intolerance, hate, and divisiveness.
  • 8Help the homeless, refugees, immigrants, and the impoverished by collaborating with other local charity organizations.
  • 9Promote education, mutual respect, and social harmony between different faiths and cultures, and create opportunities for dialogue and collaboration among people from diverse backgrounds.
  • 10Offer curricular and extracurricular programs to local youth to close their educational gap and better prepare them for college, careers, and life as contributing members of society.
