Who we are

Mission, Vision & Goals

Mission

Why we exist

We complete our mission by creating and running multihued activities and programs in six main areas: Education, Dialogue, Humanitarian aid, Advocacy, Religious and Community Services.

Vision

What we're building toward

We create inclusive programs in various areas that anyone and everyone can participate in, adding value to the Greater Boston area.

Goals

How we get there

Facilitate unity for building peace, education to eradicate ignorance, welfare to fight poverty and hunger, and progress to promote development.
